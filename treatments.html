Treatments

The care the practice offers, in summary. Which of these is right for you is a conversation to have at an appointment, not something to decide here.

Nine areas of care

From a first check-up to a full restoration.

01

Routine dental care

Check-ups designed to keep teeth and gums healthy, catch problems early, and give you confidence that your smile is in safe hands.

02

General dentistry

Preventative and restorative care to keep teeth and gums healthy for life — from endodontics to fillings, with a gentle, patient-first approach.

03

Dental hygiene

Hygiene appointments alongside routine care, as part of keeping long-term oral health on track.

04

Cosmetic dentistry

Tailored cosmetic treatment — whitening, composite bonding, veneers and complete smile makeovers.

05

Orthodontics

Straightening with Invisalign and ceramic options, for adults as well as younger patients.

06

Dental implants

Implant treatment for replacing missing teeth, and wider smile restoration.

07

Oral surgery

Surgical treatment provided at the practice rather than referred elsewhere.

08

Dental emergencies

Urgent appointments when something breaks or starts hurting — including an out-of-hours number.

09

Dentistry for children

Gentle, preventative care for children of all ages, from a first check-up to teenage orthodontics.

Paying for treatment

Finance is available.

The practice offers patient finance, including 0% options, so a larger course of treatment can be spread rather than paid in one go.

Fees depend entirely on what is involved, so they are quoted at your appointment rather than estimated in advance. The practice publishes a full price list on its own site.

Before you book

Cancellations.

  • Standard appointments — at least 48 hours’ notice. Deposits are non-refundable if less notice is given.
  • Specialist and consultant appointments — at least one week’s notice.
